# Stable NPC perception analysis, v12

The first logged WARG perception events belong to different creatures: original native array ID **948** at step 1127 and Jolt ID **952** at step 1157. Both select MOLERAT **723**. This is not a demonstrated one-second timer delay. Corresponding actors start with the same deadline and enter periodic perception at steps 1067, 1097, 1127 and 1157.

The paired diagnostic preserves the full seeded wolf scenario and stops naturally after its eighteenth recording, `w4_down`: 960 metric rows. Each backend's CSV and body-combat payloads exactly reproduce its own formal prefix. Other event fields also match except `rec_stop.speed`, which measures wall throughput. The trace covers all 101 requested steps and 57 native actor IDs in each run; no array-ID reassignment or missing snapshot step was detected. [Compact evidence and source hashes](wolf-perception-stable-analysis.json) accompany the raw records in `data/physics/stage1/wolf-v12-prefix/quad-wolf/{bullet-safe,jolt}/stable-trace`.

WARG 948 and MOLERAT 723 initially have identical positions and headings. At step 1127, the original sense-distance calculation is **2,108,982.25 cm²**, within the **2,250,000 cm²** limit; Jolt calculates **2,453,953 cm²** from already different positions and rejects the enemy before any visibility ray. The original then dispatches the enemy callback and sets target 723. The policy, deadline, interval and callback availability agree. This result does not establish a collision-query or visibility-query failure on equal inputs.

The first observed pose difference for WARG 948 occurs after `Npc::tick` at step **1074**: original heading 70.1829071°, Jolt 79.9999008°, with about 0.77 cm positional separation. Captured position, heading, policy, deadline and current target agree before that tick; no sense or visibility call for this actor occurs during it. MOLERAT 723 first diverges after tick 1077; WARG 952 after tick 1097. At step 1157, the two sight queries for WARG 952 also have different endpoint geometry: the original is occluded, while Jolt can see the molerat and acquires it.

Private go-to targets, AI queues, animation and movement-controller inputs were not traced, so matching visible pre-tick fields do not prove equal controller inputs. Two other actors already differ when the bounded trace begins. The origin of the movement difference remains unproved. No acceptance threshold, production behavior or comparator was changed.
